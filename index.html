Services / Corporate Services

Corporate services, from incorporation onward.

We facilitate every step of establishing a business in Sint Maarten, and keep your corporate records accurate and current as your enterprise evolves.

Company incorporation

Formation of your B.V. or N.V. from inception to registration: preparing the particulars, coordinating with the civil-law notary, and seeing the process through to completion.

Chamber of Commerce registration

Registration of companies, sole proprietorships and branches, together with current extracts whenever a bank, tender board or authority requires one.

Corporate amendments

Changes of directors, shareholders, registered address, trade name or business activities, recorded with the Chamber of Commerce and, where the law requires, executed through the notary.

Business licences

Applications for new licences, amendments as your activities change, and annual renewals, so your licence is never allowed to lapse.

Tax and SZV registration

Crib number and tax registration, and employer registration with SZV, leaving you ready to trade, invoice and hire.

Services / Permits & Government Liaison

Permits and government affairs, expertly handled.

From building and hindrance permits to matters before the Tax Administration or SZV, we know the procedures and the people, and we see every matter through to resolution.

Building permit applications

Preparation and submission of building permit requests, with drawings and supporting documentation in order, pursued until a decision is issued.

Hindrance permit applications

Hindrance (nuisance) permit requests for enterprises whose activities require one, submitted complete and diligently followed up.

Tax Administration matters

Assessments, penalties, outstanding filings and unclear correspondence: we establish what is required and resolve it with the Tax Administration.

SZV matters

Employer registrations, premium enquiries and disputes with Social & Health Insurances, resolved on your behalf.

Cabinet of the Governor

Matters conducted through the Cabinet of the Governor, correctly prepared and personally followed up.

Civil Registry and compliance letters

Civil Registry extracts, and the declarations and letters required by banks, tender boards and permit authorities.

Courier and document services

Secure delivery and collection of documents between government offices, banks and businesses across the island.

Privacy

How we handle your information

V.A.I.R Consultants B.V. ("VAIR", "we") handles the personal and business information our clients entrust to us with care. This page explains what we collect, why, and how we protect it.

What we collect

The documents and details you send us to carry out our services, such as identity documents, company records, financial and payroll information, and contact details. When you use the contact form we receive the details you enter.

Why we use it

Only to deliver the services you asked for: keeping your books, running payroll, preparing filings and applications, and communicating with you and with the offices involved on your behalf.

Who sees it

Our own team, and the government offices, banks or other parties that must receive it for the work you asked us to do. We do not sell or share your information for marketing.

How we store it

Documents uploaded through the Client Portal are stored in VAIR's secured Microsoft 365 environment, accessible only to our team. We keep records for as long as needed for our services and for legal record-keeping obligations.
